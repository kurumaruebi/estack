### Fixed

- WhatsApp: when another estack (on another computer or server) is linked to the same number,
  estack no longer answers that estack's posts in "Message yourself" as your requests. Before,
  the two could answer each other without end. estack now tells you in the chat, and says how to
  log out the estack that you do not use in WhatsApp's Linked devices.
- WhatsApp: when requests keep coming into "Message yourself" without a break for many minutes,
  as in a loop, estack stops answering there for 10 minutes and tells you why. A burst of messages
  or forwarded files does not do this, and "stop" still works. For a WhatsApp number on a joined
  server, the server must also be on the new version.
